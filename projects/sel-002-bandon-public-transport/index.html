01 · Overview

Research
in context.

The project investigates whether public transport can provide a credible alternative to private-car commuting and which areas may be most exposed to limited accessibility. The analysis combines network reach, scheduled service and population vulnerability.

RQ01

Is public transport a viable alternative to private-car commuting between Bandon and Cork City?

Documented as part of the project evidence workflow.

RQ02

Which areas are potentially most vulnerable to limited public transport accessibility?

Documented as part of the project evidence workflow.

02 · Study area

The geography
of the study.

Bandon is analysed as a commuter town within the Cork–West Cork transport context. Small-area population and network evidence are combined to identify spatial differences in effective access.

06 · Findings

What the evidence
shows.

Findings are stated as analytical observations. They should not be read as causal claims unless the underlying design supports causal inference.

01

Public transport is a credible option for part of the Bandon–Cork commuter market

The evidence indicates that effective access varies spatially and that some areas are better positioned to use the public transport network.

02

Stop proximity alone overstates practical access

Walking connectivity and scheduled service need to be considered alongside straight-line or stop proximity.

03

Access deficits are socially differentiated

Potential exposure to limited access is not evenly distributed across the population.

04

Equity priorities emerge from overlap

The strongest priority signal occurs where access deficits and vulnerability coincide.

08 · Interpretation

Use the evidence
with context.

Spatial analysis is a decision-support tool. Results depend on data quality, geographic scale, temporal coverage and modelling assumptions.

Interpretation principle. This project should be used as evidence for further investigation, comparison and monitoring. It should not substitute for local knowledge, detailed site assessment or stakeholder engagement.
