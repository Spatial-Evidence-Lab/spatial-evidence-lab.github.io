SPATIAL EVIDENCE LAB

About

Understanding place through spatial evidence.

Spatial Evidence Lab develops research that uses geographic data and spatial analysis to understand people, environments and places.

01 · WHAT WE DO

across
place and time.

Spatial Evidence Lab investigates how conditions vary across space and how places change over time.

Our brings together geographic information, spatial analysis, mapping and data-driven methods to examine questions relating to quality of life, accessibility, environment, climate, energy, housing, land use and population.

The Lab's work ranges from local and regional studies to national and international spatial analysis.

02 · FOCUS

Seven domains
of .

Our is organised across seven domains. The domains describe the Lab's research programme; detailed themes are used primarily to organise and discover individual projects.

01

People & Quality of Life

Understanding quality of life, living conditions and population vulnerability.

02

Accessibility, Mobility & Transport

Examining movement, accessibility and the spatial distribution of transport opportunities.

03

Cities, Regions & Land Use

Understanding how land, cities and regions develop and change.

04

Environment, Climate & Natural Hazards

Investigating environmental conditions, climate-related risks and natural hazards.

05

Energy & Infrastructure

Assessing renewable-energy potential and the spatial relationship between energy and infrastructure.

06

Housing & Socioeconomic Conditions

Examining housing, household conditions and the spatial dimensions of living conditions.

07

Population & Demography

Understanding population distribution, demographic patterns and population vulnerability.

03 · RESEARCH PRINCIPLES

Evidence-led
by design.

Spatial Evidence Lab aims to make its research understandable, documented and useful beyond the individual analysis in which it was created.

01

Evidence-led

Research questions and findings are grounded in identifiable spatial and statistical evidence.

02

Spatially explicit

Location, geographic context, scale and spatial relationships are central to our analysis.

03

Transparent

Data sources, analytical choices, assumptions and limitations should be clearly documented.

04

Reproducible

Where practical, research workflows and supporting materials are structured so that analysis can be understood and reproduced.

05

Open

We aim to share research outputs, data and analytical resources where source conditions and licensing permit.

04 · RESEARCH IN THE OPEN

Connected research,
shared openly.

Spatial Evidence Lab is built around the idea that spatial research should be understandable, documented and useful beyond the individual analysis in which it was created.

Projects, methods, data and supporting materials are developed as connected parts of the research process.
