Who is SEL and what does it investigate?

About

Independent geospatial research.

Spatial Evidence Lab is an independent geospatial research initiative using GIS, spatial analysis, data engineering and cartography to investigate how people, services, environments and infrastructure vary across places.

01 What is SEL?

Research about place.

Spatial Evidence Lab develops spatial evidence about people, places and spatial systems, with geography treated as a central part of the research question.

IDENTITY

Independent research

SEL is an independent research initiative focused on geospatial research, spatial analytics and geographic data engineering.

APPROACH

Spatial evidence

Research combines GIS, spatial analysis, open data, data engineering and cartographic visualisation to investigate geographic patterns and inequalities.

OUTPUT

Research that can be inspected

The work is structured around identifiable evidence, documented analytical choices, explicit assumptions and reproducible supporting materials where practical.

03 Geographic scope

Research across place.

SEL works across local places, Ireland and international case studies, adapting geographic scale to the question being investigated.

LOCAL PLACES

Detailed spatial questions

Small-area and local studies examine variation within towns, neighbourhoods and communities.

IRELAND

National spatial evidence

National datasets support comparative analysis across counties, regions, settlements and small areas.

INTERNATIONAL CASE STUDIES

Comparative research

Selected international studies extend the research approach to different geographic and planning contexts.

04 Research principles

Evidence with context.

These principles describe how SEL approaches evidence and communicates research, without duplicating the technical workflow described on the Methods page.

01

Evidence-led

Research questions and findings are grounded in identifiable spatial and statistical evidence.

02

Spatially explicit

Location, geographic context, scale and spatial relationships are central to analysis.

03

Transparent

Data sources, analytical choices, assumptions and limitations are documented.

04

Reproducible

Where practical, research workflows and supporting materials are structured for inspection and reproduction.

05

Open

Research outputs, data and analytical resources are shared where source conditions and licensing permit.

05 Research in the open

Follow the evidence.

Research outputs, project materials and supporting resources are published openly where source conditions permit.
