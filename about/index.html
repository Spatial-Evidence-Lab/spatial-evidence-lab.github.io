About the lab

Evidence for
better places.

Spatial Evidence Lab is an independent geospatial research initiative focused on producing rigorous, reproducible spatial analytics and geographical evidence. By combining spatial data engineering, statistical modeling and GIS visualization, the lab examines spatial disparities, service accessibility, environmental exposure, and quality of life across small areas.

Our approach

Curious,
rigorous, open.

We use spatial data to ask better questions about places - and to make the evidence behind our answers clear. Our work combines careful analysis, transparent methods and purposeful visualisation.

01Start with the questionGood spatial research begins with a meaningful question about people, places and the systems that connect them. Data helps us investigate,not define the question.
02Make the method visibleEvery indicator, assumption and transformation should be inspectable.
03Share the evidenceResearch is more useful when others can explore, question and build on it. We share our data, methods, maps and outputs wherever possible..
