About the lab

Evidence for
better places.

Spatial Evidence Lab is an independent geospatial research practice focused on spatial analytics, geographic data engineering and evidence-based planning.

01 · Approach

Curious,
careful, open.

Good spatial research should be rigorous enough to inspect and readable enough to understand. SEL therefore treats methodology, geography, data provenance and visualisation as part of the evidence—not as an afterthought.

01

Start with a place-based question

Research begins with a clearly stated spatial question and an explicit analytical geography.

02

Show the working

Indicators, transformations, assumptions and spatial operations should be documented and reviewable.

03

Share the evidence

Maps, data, methods and project documentation are structured so others can inspect and build on the work.

02 · Capability

Spatial data
to evidence.

The lab works across GIS, spatial databases, network analysis, environmental assessment, indicator construction, cartography and reproducible data workflows.

03 · Principles

Transparent by
design.

SEL projects distinguish observed spatial patterns from interpretation, and decision-support evidence from definitive decisions.

PROVENANCE

Know the source

Record dataset, geography, temporal reference and processing role.

SCALE

Respect geography

Interpret results in relation to the spatial resolution and boundaries used.

LIMITS

State uncertainty

Document assumptions, limitations and appropriate uses of each analytical result.
