About the lab

Evidence for
better places.

Spatial Evidence Lab is an independent research practice based in Cork, Ireland. We use GIS, spatial analytics and data engineering to make complex place-based questions easier to see, discuss and act on.

Our approach

Curious,
careful, open.

We believe good spatial research is both rigorous and readable. That means transparent methods, thoughtful visualisation and data that can be questioned.
