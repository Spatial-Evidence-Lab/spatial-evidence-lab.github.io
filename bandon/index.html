Spatial Accessibility Research

Accessibility to Public Transport in Bandon

Bandon, County Cork, Ireland, is a commuter town located approximately 30 km southwest of Cork City and serves as a gateway to West Cork.

This project examines spatial variations in public transport accessibility across Bandon and the relationship between accessibility and population vulnerability.

02 — Research Question

Framing the spatial accessibility problem.

RESEARCH QUESTION

How can GIS-based spatial accessibility and vulnerability analysis be used to identify and prioritise areas of transport disadvantage in Bandon, County Cork?

The study investigates whether areas with lower public transport accessibility also contain populations experiencing greater levels of socio-demographic vulnerability.

Research objectives
  • ◎ Measure spatial accessibility to public transport using GIS-based network analysis.
  • ◎ Assess the spatial distribution of population vulnerability using Census 2022 indicators.
  • ◎ Examine the spatial relationship between accessibility and vulnerability.
  • ◎ Develop a Composite Public Transport Accessibility Index.
  • ◎ Identify and prioritise potential areas of transport disadvantage.

06 — Conclusion

Interpreting the accessibility and vulnerability patterns identified across Bandon.

KEY FINDINGS

What does the analysis show?

The analysis provides a spatial assessment of how access to public transport varies across Bandon and how these patterns intersect with population vulnerability.

The Composite Public Transport Accessibility Index combines walking accessibility and service frequency, while the final hotspot analysis identifies locations where lower accessibility coincides with higher population vulnerability.

These results provide a spatial evidence base for understanding where transport disadvantage may be concentrated within the study area.

PLANNING IMPLICATIONS

Supporting transport planning

The analysis can help inform discussions about equitable public transport provision by highlighting areas where accessibility may be limited relative to population vulnerability.

  • ◎   Identify areas for further investigation of transport accessibility.
  • ◎   Support consideration of walking connections to public transport stops.
  • ◎   Inform assessment of service coverage and frequency.
  • ◎   Provide spatial evidence for prioritising potential interventions.
FUTURE RESEARCH

Extending the analysis

Future research could extend the assessment by incorporating additional dimensions of public transport accessibility and population need.

Temporal Accessibility Weekend Services Accessibility for Different Groups Real-Time Service Reliability Additional Census Indicators Scenario Analysis

Further work could also compare Bandon with other towns in County Cork and West Cork to examine whether similar accessibility and vulnerability patterns occur across different settlement contexts.
