Executive briefing

Transport access
is not evenly shared.

Bandon functions as a regional settlement and commuter node within the Greater Cork metropolitan area. Central corridors benefit from close proximity to principal stops and peak services, while peripheral estates face longer network walking distances and weaker coverage.

THE DECISION QUESTION

Where should transport planning prioritise investment when lower accessibility overlaps with greater potential transport dependence?

Accessibility and vulnerability do not follow the same spatial pattern. Older adults, people with disabilities or poor health, long-term unemployed residents and carless households may be more dependent on public transport precisely where access is weakest.

The hotspot model is a screening and prioritisation framework, not proof of unmet demand. Site-level and operational validation remains essential.

Research questions

What does
access really mean?

The study evaluates public transport as a practical alternative to private-car commuting, then considers who may be most exposed when access is limited.

RQ01

Is public transport a viable alternative to private-car commuting between Bandon and Cork City?

RQ02

Which areas are potentially most vulnerable to limited public transport accessibility because of their demographic, health, employment, or household characteristics?

The first question focuses on the spatial performance of the public transport system through walking accessibility and service frequency. The second recognises that accessibility should be evaluated within a broader social context, acknowledging that some population groups are more likely to depend on public transport than others. A bus stop may be geographically accessible, yet still fail to provide meaningful accessibility if services are infrequent or if populations with greater mobility needs remain poorly served.

Fix walking deficits

02

Optimise service frequency

Review peak-time timetable provision where stops exist but scheduled services are limited.

03

Close coverage gaps

Evaluate additional stop locations and route adjustments for underserved peripheral estates.

04
