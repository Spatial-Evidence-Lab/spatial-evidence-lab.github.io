Spatial Accessibility Research

Accessibility to Public Transport in Bandon

Bandon, County Cork, Ireland, is a commuter town located approximately 30 km southwest of Cork City and serves as a gateway to West Cork.

This project examines spatial variations in public transport accessibility across Bandon and the relationship between accessibility and population vulnerability.

02 — Research Question

Framing the spatial accessibility problem.

RESEARCH QUESTION

How can GIS-based spatial accessibility and vulnerability analysis be used to identify and prioritise areas of transport disadvantage in Bandon, County Cork?

The study investigates whether areas with lower public transport accessibility also contain populations experiencing greater levels of socio-demographic vulnerability.

Research objectives
  • ◎ Measure spatial accessibility to public transport using GIS-based network analysis.
  • ◎ Assess the spatial distribution of population vulnerability using Census 2022 indicators.
  • ◎ Examine the spatial relationship between accessibility and vulnerability.
  • ◎ Develop a Composite Public Transport Accessibility Index.
  • ◎ Identify and prioritise potential areas of transport disadvantage.

03 — Methodology

A GIS-based spatial and network analysis workflow for measuring public transport accessibility and population vulnerability.

ANALYTICAL WORKFLOW

From transport network data to accessibility–vulnerability hotspots

The methodology combines public transport network data, walking accessibility, service frequency and Census 2022 indicators through a sequence of GIS-based spatial analyses.

01

Transport Network

Public transport stops, routes and service information are assembled to represent the network serving Bandon.

02

Walking Accessibility

Walking-network distances to public transport stops are analysed to estimate access to the public transport network.

03

Accessibility Score

Walking distance is transformed into a standardised accessibility measure, with shorter distances associated with higher accessibility.

04

Peak-Time Frequency

Peak-period service frequency is incorporated to account for differences in public transport availability.

05

Vulnerability Index

Selected Census 2022 socio-demographic indicators are combined to construct a spatial measure of population vulnerability.

06

Composite Accessibility

Walking accessibility and service frequency are combined into a Composite Public Transport Accessibility Index (CAI).

07

Hotspot Analysis

Accessibility and vulnerability are combined to identify areas where lower accessibility coincides with higher vulnerability.
