01 · Research

Evidence built
for place.

SEL combines spatial data engineering, GIS, statistical analysis and cartography to turn fragmented geographic datasets into reproducible evidence. The portfolio spans local case studies, national indices and environmental analysis.

Ireland Quality of Life Index

35 indicators, seven QOL themes and small-area evidence across 18,919 Small Areas.
