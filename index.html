About Spatial Evidence Lab

Spatial Evidence Lab

Spatial Evidence Lab is an independent research initiative focused on understanding places through spatial data and geographic evidence. Founded and led by José Freire

The work combines GIS, spatial analysis, open data, data engineering and cartographic visualisation to investigate geographic patterns and inequalities.
