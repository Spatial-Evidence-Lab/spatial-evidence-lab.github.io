Spatial Evidence Lab combines GIS, spatial analytics and data engineering to investigate how people, services, environments and infrastructure vary across places.

01 · RESEARCH PROGRAMME

We investigate how people, infrastructure, land and environmental conditions vary across space and how those patterns change over time.

Measure

Turn geographic conditions into indicators, measures and comparable evidence.

Map

Reveal how patterns, concentrations and differences are distributed across space.

Understand

Interpret how geographic context influences people, places, and environmental outcomes.
