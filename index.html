About the Lab

Spatial Evidence Lab

Spatial Evidence Lab is an independent geospatial research initiative focused on producing rigorous, reproducible spatial analytics and geographical evidence.

By combining spatial data engineering, statistical modeling and GIS visualization, the lab examines spatial disparities, service accessibility, environmental exposure, and quality of life across small areas.
