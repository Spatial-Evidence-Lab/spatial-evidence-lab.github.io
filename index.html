Independent geospatial research · Spatial analysis · Geographic evidence

Research into
how places differ.

Spatial Evidence Lab investigates how geographic conditions vary between places and how those differences can be measured, mapped and understood.

We work across multiple spatial scales, using geographic data and analytical methods to examine the relationships between people, places, infrastructure and environment.
