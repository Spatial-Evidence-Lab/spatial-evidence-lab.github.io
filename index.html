Understanding places through
spatial data and geographic evidence.

Spatial Evidence Lab combines GIS, spatial analytics, and data engineering to investigate how people, services, environments, and infrastructure vary across places. Our research supports better comparisons, clearer geographic evidence, and deeper understanding of place.

04 · RESEARCH APPROACH

How do we produce the evidence?

01 Define Spatial question, study area and analytical objective.
02 Assemble Relevant datasets, boundaries and geographic inputs.
03 Construct Indicators, variables and measures suited to the question.
04 Analyse Spatial relationships, distributions, patterns and change.
05 Test Check assumptions, uncertainty, sensitivity and limitations.
06 Communicate Maps, tables, models and findings that make the evidence interpretable.
