About

José F.

Spatial Evidence Lab is an independent research initiative founded by José F., focused on geospatial research, spatial analytics and geographic data engineering.

The work explores how spatial data can be transformed into reproducible evidence about people, places and spatial systems.

Research combines GIS, spatial analysis, open data, data engineering and cartographic visualisation to investigate geographic patterns and inequalities.
