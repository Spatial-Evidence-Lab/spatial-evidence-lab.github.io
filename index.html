04 · Research Approach

From geographic question to published evidence.

A six-step workflow keeps research traceable from framing and data provenance through analysis, visualisation and publication.

01

Frame

Define the research question, geographic scope, evidence requirements and analytical objectives.

02

Source

Identify authoritative, open and derived datasets and document their provenance and limitations.

03

Engineer

Clean, normalise, integrate and spatially structure the evidence for reproducible analysis.

04

Analyse

Apply spatial analytics, network models, statistics and indicator methodologies.

05

Visualise

Translate analytical results into maps, figures, dashboards and interpretable spatial evidence.

06

Publish

Document methods and outputs so research can be reviewed, reproduced and reused.

06 · About SEL

Independent geospatial research with an evidence-first approach.

Spatial Evidence Lab brings together geographic information science, spatial data engineering, spatial analytics and visual communication to investigate how places function and change.

The platform documents the research process alongside its outputs, creating a connected evidence base of projects, methods, datasets and maps.
