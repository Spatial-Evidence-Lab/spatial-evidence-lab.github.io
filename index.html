Geospatial research · Spatial analysis · Geographic evidence

Understanding places
through spatial evidence.

Spatial Evidence Lab combines spatial analysis, GIS, data engineering and cartography to examine how people, services, environments and infrastructure vary across places. We turn geographic data into transparent, reproducible evidence by defining clear questions, using explicit geographies, documenting methods, and creating interpretable maps.

04 · RESEARCH APPROACH

From spatial question to interpretable evidence.

01 Question Define the research question, evidence requirements, and analytical objectives.
02 Geography Relevant datasets, boundaries and geographic inputs.
03 Construct Assemble relevant datasets, boundaries and geographic inputs, with their provenance and temporal reference documented.
04 Method Construct indicators, transformations, and spatial models suited to the research question.
05 Test Analyse spatial relationships, distributions, patterns, accessibility, exposure, or change.
06 Interpretation Communicate findings through maps, tables, and documented outputs, stating uncertainty, assumptions, and limitations.
